# SDED 2.0 publication candidate — September 8, 2026

Extends Substantive-Description Eponym Deprecation into an audience-relative protocol for terminology in music, philosophy, and the arts and humanities. Separates descriptive names, conventional aliases, compact symbols, contextual functions, provenance, and concept identifiers.

Adds a family-audit method, twenty-two naming failure modes, nine interventions, eighty eight-field example audits, an eight-type seventh-chord matrix, cultural and translation safeguards, an evidence review, and an empirical research agenda. The manuscript maps all eighteen requested deliverables and distinguishes verified v1.1 publication metadata from later v1.2 development sources.

Makes `("Historical name", est. YYYY)` the default attribution format. The TM marker remains acceptable but is never necessary for participation or conformance. Dates require an identified event and evidence; unknown dates do not block an initial proposal.

Supersedes universal substitution promises and layout-driven ontology. Preserves useful historical labels and symbols. Does not claim complete revalidation of the earlier vocabulary or demonstrated learning benefits.

Zenodo deposition remains pending because its authenticated pages returned HTTP 504. The historical deposited PDF downloads also timed out; those source limits are explicit in the manuscript.

## Vocabulary restoration

Restores the complete 438-entry latest Revision B vocabulary and 258 additional name/date mappings from earlier repository artifacts. The complete edition now puts the vocabulary before the extended framework paper, and a separate vocabulary-only PDF provides direct reference access. Earlier names remain visible when a semantic concern requires a correction note.

## Historical dating correction

Replaces all 82 unresolved dates in the additions with researched dates, periods, or documented bounds and records the dated event and source for all 85 additions. Corrects 14 inherited date records, preserving previous values and source provenance. Adds source notes to both PDFs and dates to the alphabetical lookup. A date-completeness check covers all 781 mapping records; additions also require matching source evidence. A full external-source audit of the remaining 682 inherited dates is still distinct from that completeness check.

Credits Wylde’s 1872 structural chord descriptions as a historical precedent. Publication rules reject blank or unknown dates while allowing contributors to submit initial proposals before research is complete. TM styling remains acceptable and optional.
