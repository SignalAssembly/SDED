# Verification of the 2.0 publication candidate

Verified September 8, 2026.

- Complete PDF: 77 pages, with the restored vocabulary before the framework paper and a linked contents page. Separate vocabulary PDF: 37 pages. Both include date evidence and correction notes; the complete edition also has a cited-source index.
- Vocabulary: 696 distinct inherited name/date mappings (438 from the latest Revision B library and 258 additional earlier mappings), plus 85 proposed new pairs. All 781 complete pairs were found in the extracted text of both PDFs in the quoted historical-name format. These are mapping counts, not a count of distinct concepts.
- Recovery reads three historical Python sources as data and four historical repository PDFs. The latest specification PDF and Revision B builder each yield the same 438 mappings. Per-artifact occurrence counts and source provenance are retained in `data/inherited-vocabulary.json`. This does not establish complete comparison with the unavailable deposited v1.0/v1.1 PDFs.
- Glyph coverage checked against embedded body and fallback fonts before generation. Extracted PDF contains no replacement characters; the optional TM clause is present.
- Every page of both current PDFs passed a text-boundary check. All 37 vocabulary pages were inspected in contact sheets. Date notes D01–D85 and correction notes C01–C14 were found in both PDFs. The contents page links to the date-evidence section. The framework's title, attribution, chord matrix, and source-index layouts were inspected during initial production; the expanded source index and revised contents were checked again.
- All 781 mapping records contain a calendar date, dated period, or documented bound. No entry retains an unknown date. Every new entry has matching date evidence and identified sources. Fourteen inherited records have explicit corrections and preserve their original dates. The remaining 682 inherited dates retain their prior-source status and have not undergone a complete external-source historical audit. Numeric completeness does not certify first use or invention.
- All 58 distinct date-source URLs are present as complete PDF links in both editions, including the URL containing parentheses. The complete PDF has 104 unique external links.
- Date research distinguishes contemporary primary documents, dated artifacts, dictionary first-known-use reports, and historical investigations. Bounds such as “by 1872” do not imply invention in that year. Unsuccessful source retrievals were not treated as inspected documents.
- Eighty audit records each contain all eight requested fields and unique proposal IDs.
- Eight chord-quality records were checked against triad interval patterns and root-relative seventh intervals. Contextual function is left unspecified rather than inferred from chord quality.
- Original artifacts are preserved. Private Drive and conversation recovery files are outside the public repository and package.
- The source archive contains 33 explicitly selected public files. All six generated vocabulary/date files reproduce byte-for-byte when rebuilt from the archive. Both PDFs and the source archive match their recorded SHA-256 checksums.
- This verifies artifact production and data consistency. It is not independent specialist review, community endorsement, or empirical validation of naming benefits.
